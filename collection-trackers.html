I built HANGAR, PADDOCK and DEPOT to keep track of my own Gunpla, diecast and Plarail collections. They’re free desktop apps where you add an item by name, and they find the photo, track what it’s worth on eBay and turn the collection into a shareable mobile-friendly catalogue.

TJ

Developer

HANGAR, PADDOCK and DEPOT started as a way to get my own collections under control: Gunpla model kits, 1:64 diecast cars and Plarail trains. All three had grown past what Notes could handle comfortably, so I built a dedicated tracker for each. They share the same core, but each has its own name, look and details that suit what it tracks: grades and scales for Gunpla, brands and castings for diecast, lines and train numbers for Plarail.

Adding an item takes just its name and what I paid. The app works out the details, finds a photo online, and keeps a running picture of the collection: what I own, what it cost, what it’s worth now and the profit or loss. Current values come from live eBay UK listings, and a wishlist with target prices keeps track of what I’m hunting for next.

The apps are also built for sharing. View mode shows the collection with all price data hidden, and one click exports it as a mobile-friendly catalogue published through GitHub Pages. All three are free, open-source Windows desktop apps built with Electron. They’re designed around how collectors actually use them: quick to add to, nice to browse, and easy to show off.
